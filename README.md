# Wallvance

### Make your screen move with you.

Wallvance is a beautiful, lightweight Android wallpaper automation app that lets you create your own rotating wallpaper experience using wallpapers already stored on your device.

No account. No cloud. No unnecessary complexity.

---

## ✨ Features

- 🖼️ Select multiple wallpapers from your device
- 🔄 Automatically rotate wallpapers
- ⏱️ Flexible wallpaper intervals
- 🎲 Random or In Order playback
- 📱 Home screen, Lock screen, or Both
- ▶️ Start / Stop automation anytime
- 🔁 Continues scheduling after device restart
- ⚡ Designed to work efficiently in the background
- 🔒 Wallpapers stay on your device
- 💬 Wallvance community for feedback and feature ideas

---

## 🎨 Built for Android

Wallvance is designed with a clean, modern Android interface inspired by Material Expressive design principles.

The goal is simple:

> Your wallpapers should feel alive without making the app complicated.

---

## 🔐 Privacy

Wallvance is designed with privacy in mind.

Your selected wallpapers remain on your device. Wallvance does not require an account or upload your wallpapers to a server.

No cloud gallery.  
No unnecessary data collection.  
No account required.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- Kotlin
- Jetpack Compose
- Material 3
- Android SDK
- Android WallpaperManager
- Android AlarmManager
- Gradle

---

## 📱 Requirements

- Android 8.0 (API 26) or higher
